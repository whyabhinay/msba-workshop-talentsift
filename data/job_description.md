# Data Analyst

**Prairie Lantern Analytics** (fictional) · Chicago, IL · Hybrid (on-site 3 days a week)

Prairie Lantern Analytics is a 20-person company that helps regional retailers and clinics make sense of their operational data. We are hiring our second Data Analyst.

## What you'll do

- Write complex SQL (joins, window functions, CTEs) against our Snowflake data warehouse
- Build Tableau and Power BI dashboards tracking KPIs for client stakeholders
- Design and analyze A/B tests, applying statistical significance testing
- Automate data cleaning and ETL workflows in Python (pandas)
- Communicate data-driven insights to cross-functional stakeholders

## Must have

- Authorized to work in the United States
- Able to work on-site at our Chicago office three days a week

## What we're looking for

- Hands-on experience with SQL
- Python or R for data cleaning and analysis
- Experience building dashboards (Tableau, Power BI, or similar)
- Working knowledge of statistics and experiment design
- Strong stakeholder communication and data storytelling

## Nice to have

- dbt or Snowflake
- Experience in retail or healthcare operations

We encourage applicants from all backgrounds to apply.
